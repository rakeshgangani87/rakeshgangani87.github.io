AI · SHOPIFY SIDEKICK

Shopify Sidekick prompts that get real store work done.

Sidekick is Shopify's AI assistant, built into the admin. Below are prompts I use and recommend, grouped by task. Click a prompt to copy it, then paste it into Sidekick and replace anything in [brackets] with your own details.

Tips for better Sidekick results

  • Be specific. Name the product, collection, date range or amount instead of saying "some" or "recent".
  • Give context. Tell Sidekick your brand tone, target customer and goal.
  • Iterate. Follow up with "make it shorter", "more casual" or "show this as a table".
  • Review before applying. Sidekick shows a preview of changes to products, discounts or themes. Always check it before you confirm.

Sidekick features vary by plan, region and language, and Shopify keeps adding new ones. Some prompts may need a newer version or Shopify Plus.
