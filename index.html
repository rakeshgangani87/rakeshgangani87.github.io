Hi, I'm Rakesh. I help brands and agencies build Shopify, Magento and BigCommerce stores that are fast, reliable and easy to grow.

I've been building for the web for over 15 years, and for the last 10 I've focused almost entirely on eCommerce. The problems I enjoy most are the messy ones: tricky migrations, B2B workflows, integrations that simply have to work, and slow stores that need to be fast.

When you work with me, you work with me directly, with no hand-offs and no account managers. I'll ask questions, explain things in plain English and tell you honestly what I'd do if it were my store.

I'm also open to white-label development. If you run an agency, I can work as your behind-the-scenes developer, under your brand and inside your own process and tools.
